TATTOO PORTFOLIO / ALBANIA

Find the direction for your next tattoo.

Explore tattoo styles, subjects and placements as you plan your next piece with London InkTattoo in Fier, Albania. From bold back compositions to botanical arm tattoos, look for the details that connect with your idea: contrast, linework, scale and the way a design follows the body.

Use these examples to start a conversation with Tisi, the artist behind the studio with 18 years of tattoo experience. Share the subject and placement you have in mind through our tattoo enquiry form. If you’re visiting Albania, include your travel dates so availability can be discussed before you make plans.

These are illustrative examples for this preview. They do not represent London InkTattoo’s original portfolio.
