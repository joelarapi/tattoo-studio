Explore tattoos by Tisi at London InkTattoo in Fier, Albania. Discover realistic portraits, large sleeves, full back pieces, traditional colour tattoos and geometric designs. Each project brings together photographs, videos where available, and a closer look at its subjects and details.

Planning your own piece? Share your idea and placement through our contact page. If you are travelling from abroad, explore our Albania visitor guide and discuss availability before arranging your trip.
