Tisi.

With 18 years of tattoo experience and an extensive body of work, Tisi creates personal tattoos at London InkTattoo in Fier, Albania. His work spans large custom pieces, detailed portrait tattoos and full sleeves, with careful attention to composition, expression and the way a design follows the body.

We take time to discuss your idea. Bring your references, a starting point or simply a feeling you want to capture. If you are unsure where to begin, we can design something for you and work through the direction, placement and details together.

07 / KNOW BEFORE YOU BOOK

A little clarity.
Before the ink.

Where is London InkTattoo?

London InkTattoo is a tattoo studio in Fier, Albania. The full address and opening hours will be added before launch. Confirm the exact studio location directly before visiting.

Who is the artist behind London InkTattoo?

Tisi is the artist behind London InkTattoo, with 18 years of tattoo experience. Visit the About Us page to get to know the artist, and use the portfolio to begin discussing your own direction.

Can I enquire from outside Albania?

Yes. Include the country you’re travelling from and your planned dates in Albania. Confirm availability and the appointment before arranging travel around your tattoo. This preview prepares a brief only; it does not send enquiries.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Request a personal estimate based on your design, detail and placement. Confirm what the quote includes, any deposit and cancellation terms before booking. No studio prices have been supplied for this preview.

Can I send reference photos or a video?

The enquiry form includes optional reference-file selection. In this preview files remain on your device and are only listed in your prepared brief. Live file delivery needs to be connected before launch.

Are the tattoos shown here studio work?

The portfolio photographs and videos show tattoos supplied by London InkTattoo. Open the project pages to explore each piece in detail.
