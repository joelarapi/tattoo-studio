05 / BEFORE YOU MAKE PLANS

A few questions.
Clear next steps.

Where is London InkTattoo?

The studio is in Fier, Albania. Tisi is the artist behind London InkTattoo, with 18 years of tattoo experience. The exact address and opening hours will be added before launch; confirm the location before visiting.

Can you help with a hotel or transport?

Tell us what assistance you need in your enquiry. Hotel recommendations and transfer coordination are proposed options in this preview, subject to studio confirmation, provider availability and an agreed price. No booking is made through this website.

Are accommodation or transfers included in the tattoo price?

No included travel services or package prices have been confirmed. Ask for separate written details of any hotel, transfer or other assistance before agreeing to it.

How many days should I stay?

That depends on the design, number of sessions and appointment schedule. Discuss your tattoo and travel dates first. This guide does not promise that any tattoo can be completed within a fixed stay.

Can I combine the appointment with a beach holiday?

Tell the artist about your intended itinerary before confirming your dates. Ask for personalised guidance about swimming, sun exposure and aftercare rather than assuming all activities will fit immediately around a tattoo session.

Can a friend or partner come with me?

Include the number of travellers when asking about accommodation or transport. Ask the studio separately about accompanying visitors during the appointment.

Does sending an enquiry confirm anything?

No. The current preview only prepares a brief on your device. A live contact channel must be connected before launch. Tattoo appointments and any travel assistance require separate confirmation.
