BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few useful answers.

Can I enquire before travelling to Albania?

Yes - prepare your idea and include your planned dates. Confirm studio availability before finalising travel. This preview does not send your enquiry.

Do I need to know the exact design?

No. A subject, a reference or a direction gives the consultation somewhere to begin. Tell the artist which details matter to you.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Ask for an individual quote based on the design and placement. Studio prices, minimum charges, deposit and cancellation terms have not yet been supplied for this site.

Is my appointment booked when I fill in the form?

No. This preview only creates a brief on your device. Once a live contact channel is connected, enquiries will still need a separate appointment confirmation from the studio.
